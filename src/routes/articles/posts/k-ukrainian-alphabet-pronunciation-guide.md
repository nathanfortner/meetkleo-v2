<h2>6. Tips for Learning Cyrillic</h2>
	<ol>
		<li><b>Start with familiar letters</b>: А, О, Т, К, М look and sound similar to English.</li>
		<li><b>Learn the "false friends" next</b>: Focus on letters that look like English but sound different (Н, Р, С, В).</li>
		<li><b>Practice with real words</b>: Don't just memorize the alphabet - learn words right away.</li>
		<li><b>Write by hand</b>: Writing helps cement the letter shapes in your memory.</li>
		<li><b>Be patient</b>: Most learners can read slowly within a week of practice.</li>
	</ol>
