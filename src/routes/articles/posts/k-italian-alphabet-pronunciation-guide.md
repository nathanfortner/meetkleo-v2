<h2>6. Tips for Italian Pronunciation</h2>
	<ol>
		<li><b>Italian is phonetic</b>: Every letter is pronounced, and the spelling tells you exactly how to say it.</li>
		<li><b>No silent letters</b>: Unlike English or French, you pronounce everything you see (except H, which is always silent).</li>
		<li><b>Master C and G rules</b>: Once you understand when they're hard vs. soft, you've conquered the biggest challenge.</li>
		<li><b>Listen for double consonants</b>: They're held longer and create the distinctive Italian rhythm.</li>
	</ol>
