	<p><span class="highlighter">"Кохаю" is reserved for romantic, passionate love</span> - the kind you express to a romantic partner.</p>
	<p><span class="highlighter">"Люблю" is for broader love</span> - family, friends, pets, and even things you enjoy (like "Я люблю каву" - I love coffee).</p>

	<h2>3. When to Use Each Expression</h2>
	<div class="wrapper">
		<span class="vline"></span>
		<span class="callout">Use "Я тебе кохаю" for:</span>
	</div>
	<ul>
		<li>Your romantic partner (boyfriend, girlfriend, spouse)</li>
		<li>Deep, passionate romantic declarations</li>
		<li>That meaningful "I love you" in a relationship</li>
	</ul>
	<br />
	<div class="wrapper">
		<span class="vline"></span>
		<span class="callout">Use "Я тебе люблю" for:</span>
	</div>
	<ul>
		<li>Family members (parents, children, siblings)</li>
		<li>Close friends</li>
		<li>Pets</li>
		<li>Things you enjoy ("Я люблю Україну" - I love Ukraine)</li>
	</ul>
	<p>Note: Some families also use "люблю" between spouses, especially after many years together. Context and family traditions matter.</p>
		<br />

	<h2>4. Pronunciation Tips</h2>
	<div class="wrapper">
		<span class="vline"></span>
		<span class="callout">The Х in "кохаю"</span>
	</div>
	<p>The letter Х makes a soft, breathy sound - like the "ch" in Scottish "loch" or German "Bach." It's gentler than a hard K or H.</p>
	<div class="wrapper">
		<span class="vline"></span>
		<span class="callout">The "лю" sound</span>
	</div>
	<p>Ukrainian Л before certain vowels becomes "soft" - almost like adding a slight Y sound. "Люблю" sounds like "lyoo-BLYOO."</p>
		<br />

	<h2>5. Other Ways to Express Affection</h2>
	<p>Ukrainians have many beautiful ways to express love and affection:</p>
	<ul>
		<li><b>Ти моє все</b> (ty mo-YE vse) - You are my everything</li>
		<li><b>Скучив/скучила за тобою</b> - I missed you (male/female speaker)</li>
		<li><b>Ти найкращий/найкраща</b> - You are the best (male/female)</li>
	</ul>

<h2>6. Cultural Note</h2>
<p>Ukrainians tend to be warm and expressive with their loved ones. <span class="highlighter">Expressions of love toward family and close friends are common and valued</span>.</p>
<p>However, like in many cultures, saying "я тебе кохаю" for the first time in a romantic relationship is a significant moment. It carries weight and meaning.</p>
