<h2>7. Tips for Mastering Spanish Pronunciation</h2>
	<ol>
		<li><b>Remember: Spanish is phonetic</b>. Once you learn the rules, you can pronounce any word correctly just by reading it.</li>
		<li><b>Focus on the unique letters</b>: J, LL, Ñ, and RR are where most English speakers need the most practice.</li>
		<li><b>Keep vowels short and pure</b>: Don't add extra sounds to vowels like we do in English.</li>
		<li><b>Practice with native audio</b>: Use the interactive examples above to train your ear and mouth together.</li>
