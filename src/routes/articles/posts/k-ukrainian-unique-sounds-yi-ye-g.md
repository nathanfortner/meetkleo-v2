	<h2>5. Why These Sounds Matter</h2>
	<p>These unique letters are part of what distinguishes Ukrainian from Russian:</p>
	<ul>
		<li><b>Ї and І</b> are used in Ukrainian where Russian uses other letters</li>
		<li><b>Є</b> appears in many common Ukrainian words</li>
		<li><b>Ґ</b> represents the true hard G sound that Ukrainian preserved from ancient times</li>
	</ul>
	<p><span class="highlighter">Using these sounds correctly shows respect for the Ukrainian language and culture</span>.</p>
		<br />

<h2>6. Practice Tips</h2>
	<ol>
		<li><b>Ї vs І</b>: Remember that Ї has two dots and adds a "Y" sound. І (without dots) is just "EE."</li>
		<li><b>Г vs Ґ</b>: Regular Г is breathy (like H). Ґ is hard (like G in "go"). Most words use Г.</li>
		<li><b>Practice Щ</b>: Say "fresh cheese" - the "sh-ch" where those words meet is similar to Щ.</li>
		<li><b>Practice Ц</b>: Say "cats" or "pizza" - you already know this sound!</li>
	</ol>
